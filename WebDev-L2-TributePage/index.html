🇮🇳 Women Freedom Fighters of India

Courage, Sacrifice, Bravery - A Tribute to the Lionesses of India

Why This Tribute?

India's freedom was not won by men alone. Women fought, sacrificed, and gave their lives for it. Some are in history books, some were forgotten. This page is a tribute to all of them.

👑

Rani Lakshmibai

1828 - 1858 | Jhansi

The Queen of Jhansi. She fought bravely against the British in the 1857 Revolt. Famous for saying "I will not give up my Jhansi."

Queen of Jhansi
⚔️

Velu Nachiyar

1730 - 1796 | Sivaganga, Tamil Nadu

The first queen to fight against the British. The Queen of Sivaganga, known as Veera Mangai - the Brave Woman.

First Queen to Fight British
🕊️

Sarojini Naidu

1879 - 1949 | Hyderabad

Known as the Nightingale of India. Poet, freedom fighter, and the first woman Governor of India (Uttar Pradesh).

Nightingale of India
🏹

Kittur Rani Chennamma

1778 - 1829 | Karnataka

She fought against the British in 1824, even before Rani Lakshmibai. One of the earliest freedom fighters.

First Freedom Fighter
🔥

Begum Hazrat Mahal

1820 - 1879 | Lucknow

She led the revolt in Lucknow in 1857 against the British. The Begum of Awadh.

Begum of Awadh
✊

Aruna Asaf Ali

1909 - 1996 | Haryana

She hoisted the Congress flag during the Quit India Movement. Known as the Grand Old Lady. Awarded Bharat Ratna.

Grand Old Lady
📻

Usha Mehta

1920 - 2000 | Gujarat

At just 22, she ran the Secret Congress Radio. She spread freedom news without the British knowing.

Radio Freedom Fighter
🙏

Kasturba Gandhi

1869 - 1944 | Gujarat

The strength behind Mahatma Gandhi. She participated in Satyagraha and Quit India Movement and went to jail.

Baa - Mother of Nation
🌸

Matangini Hazra

1870 - 1942 | Bengal

At 72 years old, she marched with the flag in her hand and died in front of British guns. Known as Gandhi Buri.

Gandhi Buri

"Without women, there is no freedom. Without their courage, there is no India."
